# Methodology

For each anchored series, the release calculates nominal and real indexes,
compound annual change, and implied half-life over the observed interval. The
half-life is a descriptive transformation of historical CAGR, not a forecast.

The workload basket combines approximately 2 vCPU / 8 GiB compute, 200 GB/GiB
general block storage, 1 TiB monthly internet egress, and current AWS public IPv4.
It is capacity-equivalent, not performance- or service-level-equivalent.

See `report.md`, `tables/analysis_manifest.json`, and `tables/source_ledger.csv`.
